Revenue in the third-quarter rose to $22.09 billion from a year earlier

Citigroup’s C -1.87%decrease; red down pointing triangle profit jumped 16% in the third quarter, powered by a surge in dealmaking and trading revenue.

The New York bank on Tuesday reported net income of $3.75 billion, or $1.86 a share. Analysts polled by FactSet expected earnings of $1.73 a share.

Revenue rose 9% from a year earlier to $22.09 billion, ahead of the $21.09 billion expected by analysts, according to FactSet.

A steady stream of sizable mergers in recent months has padded revenues for Wall Street banks, which have been eagerly awaiting the return of deals after a multiyear lull. Revenue from Citigroup’s banking unit was up 34% from the year-earlier period, with the help of a surge in corporate lending and a 17% increase in investment banking fees.

Trading revenue rose 15%, as investors continued to position for volatility from President Trump’s trade policies.

Net interest income, a measure of the bank’s core lending business, rose 12%.

Results in the quarter included a sizable goodwill impairment charge, about $726 million, or 38 cents a share, related to its sale of a stake in Grupo Financiero Banamex. Citigroup last month said that it would sell 25% of the Mexican bank as part of a plan to eventually take Banamex public.
